Frequently Answered Inquiries

Everything you need to know about Web3 storage permanence, encryption, and spatial hardware.

Where are my spatial notes physically stored? ↓

Your notes are cryptographically packaged and dispatched to the Arweave Permaweb. Arweave uses a sustainable economic endowment model designed to store data for 200+ years. Local cached copies are also stored via IndexedDB in your browser for instant offline latency.

Can the Aetheria team or node operators read my private notes? ↓

No. All text, audio recordings, and spatial coordinates are encrypted in memory on your device using client-side AES-GCM-256 before leaving your computer. The encryption keys are derived deterministically using your Web3 wallet signature (EIP-712 / Solana Keypair). Only someone with your wallet or private key can decrypt the data.

Do I need an Apple Vision Pro or VR headset to use this? ↓

Not at all! Aetheria runs natively in any standard modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Brave, Arc, Edge) on macOS, Windows, iPadOS, and Linux. The 3D canvas can be navigated via trackpad, mouse, or touch gestures. If you do own a Vision Pro or Quest 3, you can enter immersive spatial room mode with a single tap.

What happens if Aetheria as a company disappears? ↓

Because the storage layer lives on decentralized Arweave and our frontend is an open-source decentralized web app pinned to IPFS, you can load our open-source viewer from any decentralized gateway or run it locally forever. Your data cannot be turned off.

Can I export my notes into Obsidian, Notion, or Roam? ↓

Yes. Aetheria provides one-click exports to standard Markdown (`.md`), JSON-LD graph format, and `.usdz` 3D spatial models. You retain total sovereignty over your knowledge graph.
